# Overview of Workato

Workato is a leading software company specializing in enterprise automation and integration. Founded in 2013, the company provides a cloud-based platform that enables businesses to connect applications, automate workflows, and streamline operations using low-code/no-code tools.

### Workato's Mission
> To revolutionize the way organizations integrate and automate their workflows, creating a seamless and efficient workplace.

### Founders and Establishment
Workato was founded by **Vijay Tella**, **Gautham Viswanathan**, and **Harish Shetty**, with additional contributions from Dimitris Kogias and Alexey Timanovskiy. The company was officially established in **Cupertino, California**, with its headquarters now located in **Palo Alto, California**.

### Platform and Features
Workato's platform integrates applications, data, and systems across cloud-based and on-premises environments. Key features include:
- **Drag-and-Drop Interface**: Simplifies the creation of automated workflows.
- **Prebuilt Connectors (Recipes)**: Enables quick deployment of integrations.
- **Low-Code/No-Code Capabilities**: Democratizes automation for non-technical users.
- **Governance Tools**: Ensures security and compliance through Workato Aegis.
- **Conversational Integration**: Supports chat-based workflow building via platforms like Slack and Microsoft Team.

### Funding and Valuation
Workato has raised over **$420 million** across multiple funding rounds, achieving a valuation of **$5.7 billion** as of its Series E round in October 2021. Key investors include:
- Altimeter Capital
- Tiger Global Management
- Redpoint Ventures
- Battery Ventures.

## Strategic Partnerships of Workato

Workato has established numerous strategic partnerships with industry leaders to enhance its automation and integration capabilities. These collaborations focus on driving digital transformation, improving operational efficiency, and enabling seamless integration across enterprise systems.

### Key Partnerships

1. **Amazon Web Services (AWS)**  
   Workato signed a multi-year Strategic Collaboration Agreement (SCA) with AWS. The partnership focuses on scaling customer success by aligning cloud investment strategies and operationalizing AI-powered automation for joint customers. This collaboration aims to accelerate speed-to-value, enhance interoperability, and support businesses in their cloud transformation journeys.

2. **Tata Consultancy Services (TCS)**  
   Workato partnered with TCS to strengthen enterprise orchestration capabilities. This alliance helps businesses streamline operational processes, accelerate digital transformation, and embrace innovation through Workato's low-code/no-code platform integrated with TCS's domain expertise.

3. **Creatio**  
   Workato joined forces with Creatio to combine its integration platform with Creatio's no-code CRM and workflow automation solutions. This partnership enables customers to connect Creatio with over 1,500 applications, including ERPs, HR systems, and marketing platforms, providing an end-to-end no-code/low-code stack for enhanced business efficiency.

4. **Zscaler**  
   Workato partnered with Zscaler to integrate zero-trust security features into its automation workflows. This collaboration allows organizations to enforce data protection policies, block sensitive data exfiltration, and provide employee coaching on data usage through automated workflows integrated with tools like Slack and Google Workspace.

5. **BlockTXM**  
   At the Workato Partner Kick-Off 2025 event, BlockTXM reaffirmed its commitment to leveraging Workato's automation platform for seamless digital transformation. The partnership highlights shared goals of driving innovation and enhancing integration capabilities.

### Recognition by Analysts  
Workato has been recognized by Gartner and Forrester as a leader in the Integration Platform as a Service (iPaaS) segment. These accolades underscore its commitment to delivering best-in-class security, governance, scalability, and performance across its partnerships.

### Customer Distribution
- The majority of Workato's customers are based in the **United States**, followed by India and Australia.

For instance, 6sense reports that 74.79% of Workato's customers are from the U.S. Similarly, Enlyft indicates that 70% of Workato's customers are in the United States. - In terms of customer demographics, Workato's platform is utilized by companies of varying sizes. ### Challenges and Future Outlook
While Workato faces competition from platforms like MuleSoft and Zapier, its focus on innovation positions it as a leader in the integration Platform-as-a-Service (iPaaS) market. The company plans to expand its market presence further and potentially pursue an IPO in the future.
